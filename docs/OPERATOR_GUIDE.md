# Match operator guide

This guide is for the person at the official table on match day.

## Before the match

Allow time to do this before the teams warm up.

1. Plug the computer into mains power, and make sure no Windows update is waiting to restart it.
2. If you have a second screen or projector, connect it now.
3. Open the match and choose **Open match sheet & lineups**.
4. For each team: pick the players on the match sheet, choose the **Starting seven**, the **Starting goalkeeper** and the **Captain**, then add team officials A to D.
5. Fix anything HMMS lists as a problem, then choose **Confirm lineup** for that team.
6. When both teams are confirmed, the match appears under **Ready to start** in the **Live Match Centre**.

## Running the match

| Step | What to press |
| --- | --- |
| Begin | **Start match**. This locks the match sheets |
| Throw-off | **Start clock**, or the Space bar |
| Stop for any reason | **Stop clock**, or the Space bar |
| Period time runs out | The clock stops and the signal sounds |
| End the half | **End first half** |
| After the break | **Start second half**, then **Start clock** at the throw-off |
| After the last period | **Finish match** |

HMMS keeps the screen on for as long as a match is live. Keep the lid open. If the computer does go to sleep, HMMS stops the clock and does not count the time asleep. Check the time against the referees' timekeeper and restart the clock when the computer wakes.

### Recording an event

1. Select the player. Click their tile, or press **H** for home or **A** for away and type the jersey number.
2. Choose what happened.

The record panel appears as soon as a player is selected. Keys are the fastest way to work. The keys for shots and for faults, turnovers, steals and blocks need **Advanced statistics controls** switched on in Settings, which it is unless someone has changed it.

| Key | Event | Key | Event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | Goal | F | Technical fault |
| S | Shot saved | T | Turnover |
| M | Shot missed | L | Steal |
| P | Shot hit the post | K | Block |
| B | Shot blocked | U | Substitute off or bring on |
| Y | Warning | X | 2-minute suspension |
| R | Red card | Esc | Clear the selection |
| Space | Start or stop the clock | Ctrl+Z | Undo the last entry |

### Suspensions and time-outs

- Each 2-minute suspension runs on its own timer beside the team, and only counts down while the clock is running. A sound plays when it ends.
- A player's third suspension becomes a disqualification automatically.
- A red card asks whether a **Blue card** applies, which means a written report goes to the disciplinary body.
- Press the team's time-out button when it calls one. The clock stops, and the button shows how many time-outs the team has left.
- To sanction a team official, click their name under **Team officials**.

### The public scoreboard

Choose **Public scoreboard**. It opens on the second screen if one is connected, otherwise in a window you can move.

## Fixing mistakes

Nothing is ever erased. Every correction stays in the record with your name and your reason.

- **Just made a mistake:** press **Ctrl+Z**, or choose **Undo** on the message that appears.
- **An older entry is wrong:** find it in the **Match timeline**, then choose **Correct** or **Void (undo)**. You must give a reason.
- **The clock is wrong:** stop it, choose **Correct clock**, enter the right time and give a reason.

## Finishing

1. After the last period, check the score and timeline against the referees.
2. Choose **Finish match**, then **Finalize match**. HMMS writes the result from the recorded events, and takes a backup if that is switched on.
3. Open **Match report** to save or print the match sheet.

After finalizing, only an administrator can correct the match. It then shows as **Final (amended)**, and reports saved earlier are marked as out of date.

### Level at the end of a knockout match

A knockout match has to produce a winner. HMMS moves to overtime if the competition's rules include it, and then to a shoot-out. Record each throw against the player taking it, then choose **End shoot-out**. HMMS will not let a knockout match finish level.

## If HMMS stops during a match

Every recorded event is saved at the moment you record it, so power cuts and crashes lose nothing you have entered.

1. Open HMMS and sign in.
2. Open the match from the **Live Match Centre**.
3. **Recover the match clock** appears, showing the time last saved. Check it against the referees' timekeeper, then either continue from that time, or enter **Another time (MM:SS)** and choose **Use this time**.
4. Add anything that happened while HMMS was closed, using the actual match time.

If only the screen failed, HMMS reloads it by itself and the match clock keeps running. If the scoreboard window closes, choose **Public scoreboard** again.
