# User guide

HMMS is the Handball Match Management System. It runs on one Windows computer at the official table and keeps your teams, players, matches, statistics and competitions in one place.

It never needs the internet. Everything is stored on the computer it is installed on.

## Signing in

Open HMMS from the desktop icon and sign in with the username and password your administrator gave you. What you can do depends on your role:

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | Everything, including users, settings and restoring backups |
| Operator | Teams, players, officials, matches, and running live matches |
| Viewer | Look at everything, change nothing |

To change your password, go to **Settings**, **Users & access**, **Change my password**. If you forget it, an administrator can set a new one for you.

## Trying HMMS safely

On the sign-in screen, choose **Explore demonstration mode**. It opens a separate database filled with sample teams and matches, and a yellow banner stays at the top of every screen. Nothing you do there touches real data, and anything printed from it is clearly marked as demonstration data. Choose **Exit demo** in the banner to leave. It cannot be opened while a real match is in progress.

## Finding your way around

| Screen | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Live and upcoming matches, recent results, standings and top scorers |
| Live Match Centre | Run a match. The Match operator guide explains this |
| AI Vision | Follows the players and the two officials on a camera |
| Matches | Create fixtures and prepare match sheets |
| Competitions | Leagues, groups, knockouts, tables and brackets |
| Teams | Team records, colours, logos and squads |
| Players | Player records, photographs and transfers |
| Officials | Referees and table officials, and venues |
| Statistics | Player, goalkeeper and team figures |
| Match Reports | Match sheets as PDF, Excel or print |
| Match History | Every finalized match, with filters |
| Settings | Preferences, and for administrators users and backups |
| Help & guides | These guides |

## Setting up teams and players

1. Go to **Teams** and create each team, with its short name, colours and logo.
2. Go to **Players** and add players to their teams, with jersey numbers and playing positions. Mark goalkeepers as goalkeepers, because only they can be chosen as the starting goalkeeper.
3. Go to **Officials** and add your referees, table officials and venues.

Nothing with a match history is ever deleted. Archiving a team, player or official hides them from everyday lists and keeps their history. You can restore them at any time.

## Creating a match

1. Go to **Matches** and choose **Create match**.
2. Choose the two teams, the date and throw-off time, and the venue. Choose a competition if the match belongs to one.
3. Open the match and use **Open match sheet & lineups** to prepare it. The Match operator guide explains this step.

## Running a competition

1. Go to **Competitions** and create the competition. Choose its format: league, groups, groups and knockout, or knockout.
2. Add a season and register the teams.
3. For leagues and groups, choose **Generate fixtures**. You see the whole programme before anything is saved.
4. For knockout rounds, create the bracket. In a groups and knockout competition this is possible once every group match is finalized.
5. Schedule each knockout match when both of its teams are known.

Tables and brackets are worked out from finalized matches, so they are always up to date, and a corrected result reaches them automatically. If teams are level on every tie-breaker the table marks the tie. HMMS does not decide it for you: settle it under your competition's regulations.

If a correction changes who should have played in a later round after that round's match has been played, HMMS keeps the played result and flags the tie for your competition to decide.

## Reports

Open **Match Reports**, choose a match, and save it as **PDF** or **Excel**, or print it. Reports from a match still in progress are marked as provisional. Saved reports stay listed with the match, and HMMS tells you if a later correction has made one out of date.

The **Competition report** button on a competition gives the standings, knockout rounds, top scorers, goalkeepers, discipline and all fixtures. It is marked as in progress until every match is played.

## If something goes wrong

- **A screen says it hit a problem.** Choose **Reload this screen**. Everything saved is safe.
- **HMMS closes or the power fails.** Open HMMS again. Every recorded event is already saved. If a match was running, HMMS asks you to confirm the clock.

For anything else, tell your administrator. The Administrator guide covers backups and recovery.
